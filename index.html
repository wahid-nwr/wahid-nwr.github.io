05 / Engineering Approach

Architecture is about making failure manageable.

I am interested in the parts of software systems that are easy to overlook until they fail: consistency boundaries, retries, idempotency, observability, operational recovery, data integrity, and the contracts between components.

Good architecture does not eliminate failure. It makes failure visible, bounded, recoverable, and explainable.

  • Correctness before convenience
  • Explicit lifecycle and state transitions
  • Idempotency at system boundaries
  • Events treated as durable contracts
  • Observability designed into the architecture
  • Infrastructure reproducible through automation
  • Tests that exercise failure, not only success
  • Architecture decisions documented as trade-offs
